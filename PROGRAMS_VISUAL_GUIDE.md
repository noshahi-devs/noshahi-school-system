## 🔄 Hover Animation Flow

```
User hovers over card
        ↓
Card transforms (translateY(-12px))
        ↓
Shadow increases
        ↓
Border brightens
        ↓
Text remains readable
        ↓
All smooth transitions (0.35s)
```
