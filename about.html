Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution that nurtures students who are academically strong, digitally skilled, and grounded in strong Islamic values — ready to serve their families, community, and nation.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, practical education combining modern Computer Science with Nazra Quran, Tajweed, and daily character building — ensuring every student excels academically and morally.

Core Values

  • Academic Excellence & Practical Learning
  • Islamic Character & Moral Integrity
  • Safe, Clean & Supportive Environment
  • Continuous Parent Communication
  • Respect, Compassion & Discipline

Message from the Principal

"Welcome to Noshahi School System. Our goal is not just to educate, but to truly prepare each child for life — academically, morally, and practically.

In today's world, a child who can use technology confidently and carries strong Islamic values is unstoppable. That is exactly what we build here — Computer Education from Basic to Advanced as a regular subject, Nazra Quran with Tajweed, daily character training, and a safe, supportive campus environment.

Whether your child joins our School or Academy, from KG to Grade 12, they will be in the hands of highly qualified, caring teachers who communicate with parents every step of the way. We don't just teach — we build people."
