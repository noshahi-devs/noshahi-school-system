Academic Levels

Our Educational Levels

Five tailored stages of learning — each designed around the developmental needs of your child, with separate programs for School and Academy.

Level 01

Play Group & Pre-School

Nursery – KG  |  Boys & Girls

A nurturing, play-based environment where young learners develop motor skills, social confidence, basic language, and a love of learning through creative exploration and structured activities.

ABCs & Numbers Drawing & Crafts Storytelling Daily Duas Motor Skills
Enroll Now
Level 02

Primary School

Grades 1–5  |  Boys & Girls (School & Academy)

Core academic foundation — strong literacy, numeracy, and science comprehension. Students begin structured computer lab sessions and Nazra Quran with Tajweed alongside their core subjects.

English Urdu Mathematics General Science Islamiat Nazra Quran Computer Basics
Enroll Now
Level 03

Middle School

Grades 6–8  |  Girls Only (School)  ·  Boys Only (Academy)

Critical thinking deepens here. Students tackle advanced subjects including Computer (taught instead of Arabic). Tajweed and Islamic History studies continue alongside science and analytical subjects.

Physics & Chemistry Algebra History Geography Computer (as subject) Tajweed Islamiat
Enroll Now
Level 04

Secondary / Matric

Grades 9–10  |  Girls Only (School)  ·  Boys Only (Academy)

Board exam preparation with focused teaching and past paper practice. Only Computer Science program is offered at Matric level — Arts and Biology programs are not available. Computer Science replaces traditional elective streams.

English Urdu Mathematics Physics Chemistry Computer Science ✓ Pakistan Studies Islamiat
Enroll Now
Level 05

Higher Secondary

Grades 11–12  |  Boys Only (Academy)

Available exclusively at NSS Academy for boys. Advanced-level subjects with dedicated mentoring, university preparation, and a strong emphasis on practical computer science and academic performance.

Advanced Computer Science Physics Mathematics English Urdu Islamiat
Enroll Now

Student Life

Student Activities

Beyond academics — a vibrant school life that builds confidence, leadership, and community spirit.

Daily Morning Assembly

Every school day begins with a structured morning assembly — duas, announcements, and motivational talks to set a positive tone.

Naat & Qirat Competitions

Students participate in beautiful recitation and Naat competitions that nurture love for Islam and public speaking confidence.

Speech Competitions

Inter-class and inter-school speech events in Urdu and English build oratory skills, critical thinking, and confidence.

Quiz Competitions

Academic and Islamic quiz bowls that sharpen knowledge retention, quick thinking, and healthy competitive spirit.

Science Experiments

Hands-on lab experiments that make science real and exciting — students observe, test, and discover beyond the textbook.

National & Religious Celebrations

Pakistan Day, Independence Day, Eid milads, and other events celebrated with programs that build national pride and Islamic identity.

Annual Prize Distribution

A grand ceremony at the end of each academic year celebrating top performers in academics, character, sports, and co-curriculars.

Moral Training Programs

Dedicated sessions on Akhlaq, respect, honesty, and responsibility — shaping students into people of character, not just academics.
