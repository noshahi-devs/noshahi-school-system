Core Strengths

Our Specialized Focus Areas

Two pillars of excellence that define our educational philosophy and set us apart.

Core Subject

Computer Education

Computer is taught as a regular, mandatory subject at every grade level — not an optional add-on. Our 100% practical, hands-on approach focuses on real-world understanding and applicable skills.

  • Grade-tailored curriculum — from basics to advanced programming
  • Dedicated Computer Lab — with modern machines for hands-on sessions
  • Board exam ready — intensive Computer Science preparation for Matric
Explore More
Character Building

Islamic & Moral Education

We don't just teach Islamiat as a subject — Islamic values, character, and Quranic education are woven into every school day, shaping ethical, well-rounded individuals.

  • Nazra Quran with Tajweed — by certified Huffaz instructors
  • Character Report Cards — sent to parents each term for holistic development
  • Akhlaqiat & Seerah — daily practical Islamic etiquette and Quranic teaching
