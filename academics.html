Our Curriculum & Educational Levels

Comprehensive Curriculum

Our curriculum is thoughtfully designed to meet modern educational standards while integrating deep moral and technological instruction. We believe in active learning, critical thinking, and character building.

The academic structure is divided to cater to the developmental stages of our students, ensuring a smooth transition from early childhood education to secondary level mastery.

Play Group & Pre-School

Focuses on sensory-motor development and basic concepts.

Primary

Building foundational skills in languages, math, and science.

Middle

Developing critical thinking and advanced subject knowledge.

Secondary

Preparation for board exams and higher education.

Teaching Methodology

  • Interactive Learning: Encouraging student participation and practical engagement over rote memorization.
  • Technology Integration: Using digital tools and smart boards to enhance the learning experience.
  • Holistic Approach: Balancing academic rigor with emotional and ethical development.
  • Individualized Attention: Recognizing and nurturing each student's unique strengths.

Assessment System

Our assessment system is designed to provide comprehensive feedback on student progress.

  • Formative Assessments: Regular quizzes, class participation, and practical projects.
  • Summative Exams: Mid-term and final examinations to evaluate overall understanding.
  • Skill-Based Evaluation: Specific testing for computer proficiency and Islamic knowledge (Nazra and Duas).
  • Continuous Feedback: Regular parent-teacher meetings to discuss student growth.
