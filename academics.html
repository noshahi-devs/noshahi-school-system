Our Educational Programs

We offer comprehensive educational programs from Play Group to Higher Secondary, with special focus on Computer Education and Islamic & Moral Education. Each level is designed for your child's age and development.

Play Group

Age 3-4 Boys & Girls

A nurturing, play-based environment where young learners develop social skills, creativity, and early learning foundations through interactive activities and games.

  • Creative Learning through Play
  • Social Development
  • Basic Literacy & Numeracy

Nursery

Age 4-5 Boys & Girls

Building foundations of learning with structured play and academic preparation. Focus on language development, early mathematics, and kindergarten readiness.

  • Language Development
  • Early Mathematics
  • Kindergarten Preparation

Prep

Age 5-6 Boys & Girls

Primary preparation with emphasis on reading, writing, and arithmetic. Introduction to structured academics while maintaining age-appropriate play-based learning.

  • Reading & Writing
  • Mathematics Fundamentals
  • School Readiness Skills

Primary

Classes 1-5 Boys & Girls

Solid academic foundation across languages, mathematics, sciences, and social studies. Includes introduction to computer skills and Islamic education.

  • Core Academic Subjects
  • Computer Basics
  • Life Skills Development

Middle School

Classes 6-8 Boys & Girls

Enhanced academics with deeper exploration of sciences and mathematics. Strengthened computer education including MS Office applications. Character development programs.

  • Advanced Sciences & Math
  • Computer Applications
  • Islamic & Moral Education

Secondary (Matric)

Classes 9-10 Boys & Girls

Comprehensive curriculum leading to Board Examinations. Subject specialization with choice of streams. Advanced computer education and career guidance.

  • Board Examination Prep
  • Science/Commerce Streams
  • Career Guidance
