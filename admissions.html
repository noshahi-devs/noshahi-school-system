A Simple and Easy Admission Process for Your Child

Admission Process

We welcome students who want to learn and grow in a safe place where modern education and Islamic values go hand in hand. Our admission process is simple and easy — your family will feel right at home from the very start.

1

Registration

Get the registration form from our school office. Fill it with your child's and your basic details.

2

Assessment

Your child will take a simple test (Primary class and above) or have a friendly talk with the teacher (Pre-school). This helps us know your child better.

3

Parent Interview

A short meeting with you to talk about your child's education, our school rules, and answer any questions you may have.

4

Fee Submission

After everything is done, submit the required documents and admission fee to book your child's seat for this year.

Required Documents

  • Completed Admission Form (provided by school)
  • 4 Passport Size Photographs of the Student
  • Copy of Student's B-Form / Birth Certificate
  • Copy of Parents' / Guardian's CNIC
  • School Leaving Certificate (if from another school)
  • Previous Result Card (if applicable)
  • Medical/Health Certificate (optional)

Eligibility Criteria

Play Group:

3+ Years (as of July 2026)

Nursery:

4+ Years (as of July 2026)

Prep:

5+ Years (as of July 2026)

Higher Classes:

Age should match the class based on last school result

Essential Details

General Information

Everything you need to know about fees, uniforms, timings, and transport — all in one place

Fee Information

Low monthly fees that cover study and activities. Full fee details given at our office.

  • Monthly fee due by 10th of each month
  • Sibling discount available for 2nd child

Uniforms

Both summer and winter uniforms are needed. Shop details given when your child joins.

  • Available at designated shops near school
  • PE uniform for sports activities

School Timings

Mon-Thu: 8:00 AM - 1:30 PM | Fri: 8:00 AM - 12:00 PM

  • Gate opens at 7:30 AM sharp
  • Assembly starts at 7:50 AM

Transportation

Bus service is available on many routes. Charges are shared when you apply.

  • Covers major areas of the city
  • GPS tracked for parent peace of mind
