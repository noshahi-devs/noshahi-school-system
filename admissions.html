Admission Process, Schedule Timings & Required Information

Admission Process & Procedure

To apply for admission, parents or guardians are requested to visit the Admission Office during school hours. Our admission team is ready to guide you through the process.

1

Office Visit

Visit the Admission Office during school timings.

2

Document Submission

Submit all required documents to the Admission Officer.

3

Form Completion & Review

Our Admission Officer will complete and review the admission form with the parent/guardian.

4

Signature & Final Approval

The completed form must be signed by the parent/guardian and the Principal. Once approved, the admission process will be completed.

Note: Admission charges will be applicable as per the Fee Structure.

Required Documents

Please bring the following documents when visiting:

  • Photocopy of the student's B-Form
  • Photocopy of the Father's CNIC
  • Home Contact Number
  • Home WhatsApp Number
  • Four (4) recent passport-size photographs of the student
  • School Leaving Certificate (if applicable)
