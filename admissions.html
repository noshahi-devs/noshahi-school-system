Admission Process

We welcome students who are eager to learn and grow in a balanced environment of modern technology and Islamic values.

1

Registration

Obtain and submit the registration form from the campus admission office.

2

Assessment

The student will undergo a basic assessment test (for primary and above) or an interview (for pre-school).

3

Parent Interview

A brief meeting with the parents to align on educational goals and values.

4

Fee Submission

Upon clearance, submit the required documents and admission fee to secure the seat.

Required Documents

  • Completed Admission Form
  • 4 Passport Size Photographs of the Student
  • Copy of Student's B-Form / Birth Certificate
  • Copy of Parents' / Guardian's CNIC
  • School Leaving Certificate (if applicable)
  • Previous Result Card (if applicable)

Eligibility Criteria

Play Group: 3+ Years

Nursery: 4+ Years

Prep: 5+ Years

For higher classes, age should correspond appropriately with passing the previous grade.
