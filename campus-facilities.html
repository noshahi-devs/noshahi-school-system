Campus Facilities

A Safe and Inspiring Environment for Learning

Modern Classrooms

Spacious, well-ventilated classrooms equipped with comfortable seating and smart boards to facilitate interactive learning.

Computer Lab

State-of-the-art computer labs with high-speed internet and the latest software, essential for our core computer education focus.

Science Lab

Fully equipped laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, and Biology to encourage practical experiments and scientific inquiry.

Library

A rich repository of books ranging from modern sciences and literature to extensive Islamic texts and historical accounts.

Prayer Area

A dedicated, serene prayer area where students offer daily prayers and learn practical aspects of Islamic worship.

Playground

Safe outdoor spaces designed for physical activities, morning assemblies, and recreational breaks to ensure physical well-being.

Transport

Safe and reliable transportation services covering major routes, ensuring students commute comfortably to and from school.

Security

24/7 CCTV surveillance, restricted access, and trained security personnel to ensure a perfectly safe campus environment.
