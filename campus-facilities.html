Core Program

Dedicated Computer Lab

Fully equipped with modern machines for hands-on, practical Computer Education — Basic to Advanced. Computer is a mandatory regular subject at every grade, not just an extra class.

CCTV Security System

Full campus surveillance throughout the school day. Complete peace of mind for every parent.

Clean & Hygienic Environment

Every classroom, corridor, and common area maintained spotless to the highest daily standards.

Nazra Quran with Tajweed

Structured Quran classes by certified Huffaz — correct recitation, Tajweed rules, and a lifelong bond with the Quran.

Spacious Private Building

A purpose-built campus giving students ample, comfortable space to learn and grow every day.

Continuous Parent Communication

Parents are involved at every step — regular meetings, term reports, Character Report Cards, and open-door teacher access ensure you are always informed about your child's progress.

Academic & Moral Training

Special focus on both academic excellence and character — Tarbiat sessions, Akhlaqiat lessons, and daily etiquette practices shape students who are knowledgeable and principled.

Clean Drinking Water

Filtered, safe water available throughout the campus at all times — student health is never compromised.

Highly Qualified Staff

Supportive Learning Environment

A warm, safe atmosphere where every student feels valued, motivated, and confident to learn and grow.

High-Quality Character Building
