Learning Highlights

Comprehensive Computer Curriculum

Each feature is carefully designed to build real digital skills through practical, engaging, and progressive learning.

Computer Education Starts Early

Computer education begins from the early grades, allowing students to become familiar with technology from a young age.

Practical Computer Lab Training

Students regularly participate in hands-on sessions where they learn by practicing on computers.

Basic Computer Knowledge

Students understand computer fundamentals including hardware, software, operating systems, and digital awareness.

Microsoft Word

Students learn document creation, editing, formatting, and printing using Microsoft Word.

Microsoft PowerPoint

Students create presentations using slides, images, animations, and visual elements.

Basic Typing Skills

Regular typing practice improves typing speed, keyboard familiarity, and accuracy.

Introduction to Modern Technology

Students explore the internet, digital tools, and responsible technology usage.

Future Education & Career Preparation

The program develops digital skills that prepare students for higher education and future career opportunities.

Learning Journey

Student Learning Path

A progressive roadmap designed to take students from basics to advanced digital skills step by step.

Step 01

Computer Basics

Understanding computer hardware, software, and operating systems — the foundation of digital literacy.

Step 02

Typing Skills

Building speed and accuracy through regular typing practice and keyboard familiarity exercises.

Step 03

MS Word

Document creation, editing, formatting, page layout, and printing skills for academic and personal use.

Step 04

MS PowerPoint

Creating engaging presentations with slides, images, animations, and visual storytelling.

Step 05

Digital Skills

Internet safety, email communication, cloud tools, and responsible technology usage for modern life.

Step 06

Future Ready

Advanced digital skills, project-based learning, and preparation for higher education and careers.
