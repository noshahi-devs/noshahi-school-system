Learning Highlights

What Your Child Will Learn

Your child will learn real computer skills that help them in school and in life — step by step, at their own pace.

Early Start with Computers

Your child starts learning computers from a young age, so they get comfortable with technology early on.

Hands-On Practice in Lab

Every class includes real practice on computers — your child learns by doing, not just watching.

Computer Basics

Your child will learn how computers work — what's inside, how to use them, and how to stay safe online.

Microsoft Word

Your child will learn to write, edit, and print documents — a skill they'll use throughout school and beyond.

Microsoft PowerPoint

Your child will create colorful presentations with pictures and animations — great for school projects.

Typing Skills

Regular typing practice helps your child type faster and more accurately — a skill they'll use every day.

Safe Internet Use

Your child will learn how to use the internet safely and responsibly — knowing what's right and what to avoid.

Ready for the Future

These computer skills prepare your child for higher studies and future careers in a digital world.

Learning Journey

Student Learning Path

A progressive roadmap designed to take students from basics to advanced digital skills step by step.

Step 01

Computer Basics

Understanding computer hardware, software, and operating systems — the foundation of digital literacy.

Step 02

Typing Skills

Building speed and accuracy through regular typing practice and keyboard familiarity exercises.

Step 03

MS Word

Document creation, editing, formatting, page layout, and printing skills for academic and personal use.

Step 04

MS PowerPoint

Creating engaging presentations with slides, images, animations, and visual storytelling.

Step 05

Digital Skills

Internet safety, email communication, cloud tools, and responsible technology usage for modern life.

Step 06

Future Ready

Advanced digital skills, project-based learning, and preparation for higher education and careers.

Career Readiness
