Our Academic Programs

Play Group & Primary

Nursery to Grade 5 — open to Boys & Girls in both School and Academy. Foundational academics, Nazra Quran, and Computer basics from day one.

Middle School

Grades 6–8. Girls Only at School · Boys Only at Academy. Computer is taught as a core subject (replacing Arabic) alongside all standard subjects.

Secondary & Higher Sec

Grades 9–10 (School & Academy) · Grades 11–12 (Academy only, Boys). Only Computer Science at Matric — Arts and Biology programs are not offered.

Focus on Computer Education

Computer is not an optional add-on — it is a regular, mandatory subject at every grade level. Our approach is 100% practical, built around real learning — not theory-heavy rote memorization.

  • Computer education from Basic to Advanced level
  • Curriculum tailored to each student's age and grade
  • Practical, hands-on sessions in a dedicated Computer Lab
  • At Middle level: Computer taught instead of Arabic
  • At Matric: Only Computer Science — Arts & Biology not offered
