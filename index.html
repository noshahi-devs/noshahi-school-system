Why Choose Us

Computer as Main Subject

Computer is taught like Math or Science - a real subject, not extra. Kids learn by doing, not just reading. We teach from easy to hard, based on what grade they are in.

Quran & Good Behavior

Every day kids read Quran with correct voice. We teach them how to say prayers, good manners, and about Islam. This helps them be good people and good students at the same time.

Safe & Clean Place

Our school is very clean. We have cameras everywhere to keep watch. Water is clean. Teachers are good and caring. We talk to parents every week. Your child is safe here.

Good & Trained Teachers

Our teachers know a lot. Teachers for Quran can read it very well. Teachers for Computer know how computers work. We teach kids to understand things, not just remember.

Fun Games & Events

Every day we have a morning meeting. We have Quran reading games, speaking games, thinking games, and science work. We have big parties for country days and give prizes at the end of year.

Our Own Big Building

We have our own building. All things are there. Computer room, nice classrooms, good play area. Everything is made for kids to learn well and grow up strong.

Our Academic Programs

Foundation

Nursery to Grade 5

Both boys and girls can join. Kids learn all main subjects - reading, math, science. They read Quran every day. Computer class starts from here too. We teach slowly and fun.

Growth

Grade 6 to 8

In school - only girls. In academy - only boys. Now computer is very important - we teach it like math. Kids learn more hard things. Same good teachers, same good care.

  • Advanced subjects
  • Computer as main subject
  • Specialized teachers
Excellence

Grade 9 and Up

In school and academy - grade 9 and 10. In academy only - grade 11 and 12 for boys. We only teach computer science here. No other choices. Just computer, and computer only.

  • College preparation
