Why Choose Us

Islamic Education & Tarbiat

Nazra Quran with Tajweed, daily Duas, Akhlaqiat, and Islamic History — woven into every school day to build character alongside academics.

Safe & Supportive Environment

Clean, hygienic premises with CCTV security, qualified staff, pure drinking water, and continuous parent communication — your child is in safe hands.

Experienced, dedicated teachers including certified Huffaz for Quran and specialist computer instructors who focus on real understanding, not rote learning.

Our Academic Programs

Play Group & Primary

Nursery to Grade 5 — open to Boys & Girls in both School and Academy. Foundational academics, Nazra Quran, and Computer basics from day one.

Middle School

Grades 6–8. Girls Only at School · Boys Only at Academy. Computer is taught as a core subject (replacing Arabic) alongside all standard subjects.

Secondary & Higher Sec

Grades 9–10 (School & Academy) · Grades 11–12 (Academy only, Boys). Only Computer Science at Matric — Arts and Biology programs are not offered.

Focus on Computer Education

Computer is not an optional add-on — it is a regular, mandatory subject taught at every grade level. Our approach is 100% practical, not theory-heavy.

  • Computer education from Basic to Advanced level
  • Age & grade-appropriate curriculum for every class
  • Practical, hands-on learning — not theory only
  • At Middle level: Computer replaces Arabic (not Quran Translation)
  • At Matric: Full Computer Science program (Arts & Biology not offered)
