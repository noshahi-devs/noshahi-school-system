Our Curriculum

The Three Pillars

Each pillar is a full area of learning — well planned, meaningful, and taught with care.

Pillar 01

Hifz & Nazra

Students learn to read and memorize the Quran with proper pronunciation. Our trained teachers follow a step-by-step plan so every child builds a deep and lasting bond with the Quran.

Hifz Program Tajweed Rules Nazra Quran Makharij
Pillar 02

Akhlaqiat & Tarbiat

Good character is built every day — through morning prayers, kind greetings, honesty habits, and manners training. Students learn to respect parents, teachers, and friends as part of their daily life.

Daily Duas Respect & Adab Honesty Etiquette
Pillar 03

Islamic History

Children learn the inspiring stories of the Prophets, companions of the Prophet (SAW), and great Islamic scholars. These heroes of history spark dreams, purpose, and a proud Islamic identity that lasts a lifetime.

Seerah Sahaba Stories Islamic Heroes Khulafa

Islamic Values
Trained Quran Teachers All Quran classes are taught by trained teachers who know Tajweed well and have experience teaching children.
Daily Duas & Prayers in Real Life Students start every school day with group Fajr duas and learn daily prayers by heart through regular practice.
Good Behavior Report Cards Parents get a special Behavior & Tarbiat report every term — showing how their child is doing in manners, coming on time, and Quran progress.
Safe and Caring Place School culture built on respect and Islamic values — bullying is not allowed and kindness is always rewarded.
Monthly Meetings with Teachers Regular meetings about your child's character and religious growth — not just their grades.
Islamic Programs and Competitions Students take part in Naat competitions, Islamic quizzes, and Ramadan programs that build pride in their faith.
