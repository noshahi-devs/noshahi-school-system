Our Curriculum

The Three Pillars

Every pillar is a complete world of learning — structured, purposeful, and delivered with love.

Pillar 01

Hifz & Nazra

Students memorize and recite the Quran with correct Tajweed. Our certified teachers follow a structured, age-appropriate program ensuring each child builds a strong and lasting connection with the Quran.

Hifz Program Tajweed Rules Nazra Quran Makharij
Pillar 02

Akhlaqiat & Tarbiat

Character is built daily — through morning duas, respectful greetings, honesty exercises, and etiquette workshops. Students learn to respect parents, teachers, and peers as a lived practice, not just a lesson.

Daily Duas Respect & Adab Honesty Etiquette
Pillar 03

Islamic History

Children discover the inspiring stories of the Prophets, Sahaba, and great Islamic scholars. These heroes of history ignite ambition, purpose, and a sense of Islamic identity that lasts a lifetime.

For Parents

Your Peace of Mind, Guaranteed.

We know parents have one question above all else: "Is my child really being shaped with the right values?" Here's our answer.

Qualified Huffaz Teachers All Quran classes are conducted by certified Huffaz with formal Tajweed qualifications and experience teaching children.
Daily Practical Duas & Prayers Students begin every school day with collective Fajr duas and learn age-appropriate daily prayers by heart through consistent practice.
Character Report Cards Sent to Parents Beyond academics, parents receive a dedicated Character & Tarbiat report each term — documenting Akhlaq, punctuality, and Quran progress.
Safe & Nurturing Environment Our school culture is built on respect, brotherhood, and Islamic values — bullying has zero tolerance and kindness is actively rewarded.
Monthly Parent-Teacher Meetings Regular meetings specifically covering your child's spiritual and moral development — not just their grades.
Islamic Events & Competitions Students participate in Naat competitions, Islamic quizzes, and Ramadan programs that build identity and pride in their deen.
