About Noshahi School System

Who we are and what we stand for

Noshahi School System provides modern academic education with a special focus on Computer Education and Islamic character building, from KG to Grade 12. We believe every child deserves a safe, colourful, and encouraging place to learn — one that blends strong fundamentals with practical, real-world skills and firm moral values.

Our Core Values

Academic Excellence Islamic Character & Moral Integrity Safe & Supportive Environment Continuous Parent Communication Respect, Compassion & Discipline
Computer as a Core SubjectTaught at every grade level, basic to advanced, in our dedicated computer lab.
Nazra Quran with TajweedDaily Duas and Akhlaqiat alongside academic learning.
Highly Qualified StaffCertified Huffaz and specialist teachers focused on real learning.
CCTV & Clean CampusA secure, clean, and disciplined environment every school day.

Rules & Discipline

Our friendly code of conduct for every student

  1. 1 Punctuality & regular attendance; leave application required for planned absence.
  2. 2 Complete summer/winter uniform, including PE kit, mandatory at all times.
  3. 3 Courteous, respectful behaviour towards staff and fellow students.
  4. 4 Homework and class work completed and submitted on time.
  5. 5 Care of school property, books, and equipment.
  6. 6 Monthly fee paid before the 10th of each month.
  7. 7 Personal mobile phones/gadgets not permitted without prior permission.
  8. 8 Parents expected to attend PTMs and respond to school communication.
A detailed Code of Conduct handbook is shared with parents at the time of admission.
