Admission Process & Requirements

To apply for admission, parents or guardians are requested to visit the Admission Office during school hours.

1

Office Visit

Visit the Admission Office during school timings.

2

Document Submission

Submit all required documents to the Admission Officer.

3

Form Completion & Review

Our Admission Officer will complete and review the admission form with the parent/guardian.

4

Signature & Final Approval

The completed form must be signed by the parent/guardian and the Principal. Once approved, admission is complete.

Required Documents

  • Photocopy of the student's B-Form
  • Photocopy of the Father's CNIC
  • Home Contact Number
  • Home WhatsApp Number
  • Four (4) recent passport-size photographs of the student
  • School Leaving Certificate (if applicable)

Code of Conduct

Rules & Discipline

Every student is expected to uphold the following standards to maintain a positive, safe, and productive learning environment.

01

Punctuality & Attendance

Students must arrive on time and maintain regular attendance. Prior leave application is required for planned absence.

02

School Uniform

Complete summer/winter uniform, including PE kit on designated days, is mandatory at all times.

03

Respect & Behaviour

Courteous conduct towards teachers, staff, and fellow students is expected at all times, on and off campus.

04

Homework & Academics

Assigned homework and class work must be completed regularly and submitted on time.

05

Campus Property

Students are responsible for the care of school property, books, and equipment issued to them.

06

Fee Payment

Monthly fee must be paid before the 10th of each month to avoid late charges or suspension of classes.

07

Mobile & Electronic Devices

Personal mobile phones and electronic gadgets are not permitted on campus without prior permission.

08

Parent Communication

Parents/guardians are expected to attend PTMs and respond promptly to school communication.

A detailed Code of Conduct handbook is shared with parents at the time of admission.

Session Planning

Academic Calendar

A general overview of how the academic year is structured at Noshahi School System.

Session Structure

The academic year is divided into two terms, each concluding with a formal assessment period.

Assessments

Regular monthly tests, a Mid-Term examination, and an Annual/Final examination are held each session.

Parent-Teacher Meetings

PTMs are held periodically to discuss student progress and share report cards directly with parents.

Vacations

Summer and winter breaks are observed as per the official school calendar and government directives.

Islamic & National Days

Islamic and national occasions are observed with special assemblies, Naat/Qirat, and commemorative activities.

Annual Prize Distribution

An annual ceremony recognizes outstanding academic and co-curricular achievement across all grades.

Exact dates are announced via official circulars/notice board at the start of each academic session — contact the school office for the current calendar.
