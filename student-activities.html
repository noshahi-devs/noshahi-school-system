Islamic Activities

Religious Activities & Moral Values

We nurture strong Islamic values through various religious competitions and activities that help students develop a deep connection with their faith.

Naat Competition

Students recite beautiful Naats to express their love for Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) and develop a connection with Islamic traditions.

Qirat Competition

Students showcase their beautiful recitation of the Holy Quran with proper Tajweed, encouraged by our certified Quran teachers.

Islamic Quiz

Students test their knowledge of Islamic history, Quran, Hadith, and important events through engaging quiz competitions.

National Celebrations

National Day Celebrations

We celebrate Pakistan's important national days with great enthusiasm to build patriotism and awareness among students.

Independence Day

We celebrate 14th August with flag hoisting, national songs, and patriotic speeches by students.

Pakistan Day

23rd March is celebrated with special assemblies, tableaux, and speeches highlighting Pakistan's history.

Iqbal Day

Students honor Allama Iqbal through poetry recitations, speeches, and essays about his vision for Pakistan.

Quaid-e-Azam Day

We celebrate 25th December with special events remembering the founder of Pakistan, Muhammad Ali Jinnah.
